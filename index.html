Welcome

Advent 2026 Reflection Guide

Advent comes from the Latin word adventus, meaning “arrival” or “coming.” For centuries, followers of Jesus have spent the weeks before Christmas doing two things: remembering Jesus’ first arrival in history, and looking ahead to his final arrival, when he will return to defeat evil and make all things well.

The Bible tells one long story. From the very beginning, after humanity turned away from God, God promised to send a Rescuer. These readings trace that promise: through Abraham and David, through the prophets who spoke in times of war and exile, to John, Mary, and Joseph, and finally to a manger in Bethlehem.

Each day has one short Scripture reading and a brief explanation. The passage is the focus. The explanation is simply there to connect it to the Big Story of God’s rescue.

Read the passage together with your family, housemates, or friends. Choose a set time, like first thing in the morning or right after dinner. It only takes a few minutes.

After each reading, reflect, then pray
  • What does this show us about God?
  • How does it point to Jesus?
  • What does this make you want to pray?
With young children, start simpler
  • What do you think this Bible passage is saying?

Join us this Advent as we wait together as a church, seeking the One who came and who is coming again.
